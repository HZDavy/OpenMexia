常见问题Frequently asked

安装时 SmartScreen 提示「已保护你的电脑」？SmartScreen says “Windows protected your PC”?
本软件是个人开源项目，暂未购买代码签名证书。点击「更多信息」→「仍要运行」即可继续安装。软件完全开源，可自行审计源码或从源码构建。The app is an unsigned personal open-source project. Click “More info” → “Run anyway”. The source is fully open and auditable.
米家账号和设备数据安全吗？Is my Mi Home account safe?
应用走米家官方扫码登录，不接触你的账号密码。登录凭据与设备数据仅保存在本机应用数据目录（userData），直接与米家官方接口通信，不经过任何第三方服务器。It uses the official Mi Home QR login and never sees your password. Credentials and device data live only in the local userData folder and talk directly to Mi Home.
为什么我的设备没有控制项？Why does a device have no controls?
控制项按设备的标准 MIoT 规格自动生成。部分老型号或第三方品牌设备未提供标准规格，会只显示状态。随着更多协议与厂商开放接口的接入，支持范围会持续扩大。欢迎到 GitHub Issues 反馈设备型号。Controls are generated from the device's standard MIoT spec. Some older or third-party models expose no spec and show status only. Support will keep expanding as more protocols and vendors' open APIs are integrated. Report the model on GitHub Issues.
软件收费吗？会收集数据吗？Is it free? Does it collect data?
完全免费、开源，没有内购、广告和任何统计追踪。你也可以自行从源码构建可执行文件。Completely free and open source — no purchases, ads or analytics. You can build the executable yourself.
会支持哪些协议的设备？Which device protocols will it support?
凡是能以开放方式访问和控制的设备，都是 OpenMexia 的目标对象——不限于小米，也不限于某一个协议。无论是厂商公开的接口、社区维护的接入方案，还是本地局域网协议，只要能以开源方式接入，都会尽力支持。目前已支持米家 MIoT，Matter 等更多协议与厂商开放接口将逐步纳入。Any device that can be accessed and controlled in an open way is a target for OpenMexia — not limited to Xiaomi or to a single protocol. Whether it's a vendor's public API, a community-maintained integration, or a local network protocol, we aim to support it as long as it can be reached through open means. Mi Home MIoT is supported today; Matter and more protocols and vendor APIs will follow.
会支持 macOS、iOS、Android、watchOS、Wear OS、Linux、HarmonyOS 吗？Will it support macOS, iOS, Android, watchOS, Wear OS, Linux and HarmonyOS?
会，多平台一致体验是 OpenMexia 的核心方向。Windows 版（基于 Electron）已经发布，macOS 是下一个目标；iOS、Android 也在路线图上，计划复用同一套设备核心，让卡片与交互在各平台保持一致。面向 Apple Watch 的 watchOS、面向 Android 手表的 Wear OS，以及 Linux、HarmonyOS 同样在规划之中。在协议层面，目标是支持所有智能协议、任何品牌，不只小米，目前已完成米家 MIoT 的接入，后续还将逐步纳入 Matter 等更多协议与厂商开放接口。进展与时间点请关注 GitHub Issues。Yes — one consistent experience across platforms is the core direction. The Windows build (Electron-based) is out now, macOS is next, and iOS & Android are on the roadmap, sharing one device core so the cards and interactions stay consistent. watchOS for Apple Watch, Wear OS for Android watches, plus Linux and HarmonyOS, are also planned. On the protocol side, the goal is to support every smart-home protocol and any brand, not just Xiaomi; Mi Home MIoT is supported today, and Matter plus more protocols and vendors' open APIs will follow. Follow the progress on GitHub Issues.
